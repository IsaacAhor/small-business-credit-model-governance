# Model Governance Checklist

Use this checklist to document whether an ML-based small business credit underwriting model has the minimum governance controls expected for disciplined oversight.

## 1. Scope and Ownership

- Has the model purpose been clearly documented?
- Has the covered product or portfolio been clearly defined?
- Is the underwriting decision stage documented?
- Is the model role advisory, determinative, or hybrid?
- Is there a named business owner?
- Is there a named technical or model owner?
- Are challenge, compliance, and review responsibilities identified?

## 2. Documentation

- Is there a model description document?
- Are inputs, outputs, assumptions, and limitations documented?
- Is data status documented as synthetic, proxy, public, or production?
- Is data provenance and permitted use documented?
- Is the training or development approach documented?
- Are change logs and version history maintained?
- Are approval records preserved?

## 3. Validation and Review

- Has independent review or challenge occurred?
- Are validation findings documented?
- Are known limitations tracked?
- Are threshold sensitivity and override behavior reviewed?
- Are remediation items assigned and dated?

## 4. Explainability Controls

- Is the explanation approach documented?
- Is reason-code or explanation mapping defined?
- Has explanation consistency been reviewed?
- Are adverse-action reason categories mapped to model or policy logic?
- Are explanation stability checks repeated after material changes?
- Are explanation limitations disclosed for governance purposes?

## 5. Monitoring

- Are performance metrics defined?
- Are drift indicators defined?
- Are explanation drift or reason-code mix indicators defined?
- Are threshold breaches linked to escalation actions?
- Is monitoring cadence defined?
- Is exception tracking in place?

## 6. Fair-Lending and Compliance Review

- Are disparity monitoring methods defined?
- Are review segments clearly documented?
- Are proxy-variable risks identified and reviewed?
- Are escalation triggers defined for fairness concerns?
- Is there a path for less discriminatory alternative assessment when indicated?

## 7. Change Management

- Are retraining or redevelopment triggers defined?
- Are model updates subject to approval?
- Are threshold, reason-code, data-source, and segmentation changes reviewed?
- Is post-change review documented?
- Are retired versions archived appropriately?

## 8. Reporting

- Is management reporting cadence defined?
- Are unresolved issues reported to the right oversight level?
- Is remediation progress tracked?

## Notes

This checklist is a template, not a legal opinion or institution-specific policy substitute. It should be adapted to the relevant governance environment and documentation standard.
