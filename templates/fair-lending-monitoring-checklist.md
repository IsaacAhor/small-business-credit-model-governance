# Fair-Lending Monitoring Checklist

Use this checklist to structure periodic review of potential fair-lending concerns in ML-based small business credit underwriting.

## 1. Monitoring Scope

- Is the monitored decision population clearly defined?
- Are segments and comparison groups documented?
- Are exclusions and limitations documented?
- Is the underwriting decision stage identified?
- Are policy overlays, overrides, or manual reviews in scope?

## 2. Data Readiness

- Are the required variables available and consistently defined?
- Are missing-data patterns reviewed?
- Are proxy limitations or synthetic-data conditions disclosed where relevant?
- Is the data source, permitted use, and sensitivity classification recorded?
- Are protected-class proxy risks reviewed for key features?

## 3. Outcome Review

- Are approval, denial, pricing, or other decision outcomes being tracked?
- Are disparities reviewed at meaningful intervals?
- Are threshold rules documented?
- Are adverse-action reason distributions reviewed by segment where relevant?
- Are override and exception trends reviewed by segment?

## 4. Explanation Review

- Are explanation outputs or reason codes being sampled and reviewed?
- Are explanations understandable and consistent with the underlying decision logic?
- Are unstable or contradictory explanations flagged?
- Are reason-code mappings reviewed after threshold or model changes?
- Are explanation limitations reported to governance reviewers?

## 5. Alternative Assessment

- Are trigger conditions defined for less discriminatory alternative review?
- Is there a documented process for comparing alternatives?
- Are tradeoffs between predictive performance and disparity reduction documented?
- Are alternatives reviewed when recurring disparity signals appear?
- Is the acceptance or rejection of an alternative documented?

## 6. Escalation and Remediation

- Are fairness concerns assigned to a review owner?
- Are escalation thresholds documented?
- Are remediation actions tracked to closure?
- Are accepted risks documented with rationale and approval?
- Are repeat findings linked to prior issues or monitoring reports?

## 7. Reporting

- Are findings summarized for governance or management review?
- Are repeat issues tracked over time?
- Are unresolved items elevated appropriately?

## Notes

This template is intended as a practical monitoring aid. It does not replace legal review, institutional policy requirements, or jurisdiction-specific compliance guidance.
