# Practitioner assessment

Assess whether selected model-governance artifacts help a practitioner trace
an adverse-action reason, recognize a review gap, and decide what to examine
next. Work with prepared synthetic records. No Python installation or software
execution is required.

## Agree a bounded assignment

Use the [common agreement](protocol.md#target-and-common-agreement) and
[select an exact review target](current.md). Its target sheet
provides pinned reading and task links, followed by disclosed limitations.
Agree the review context, included artifacts, deliverables, and effort cap.
A cap of two to four hours can be proposed for a first assessment; this is an
untested planning allowance, not a measured completion time. Narrow the tasks
if necessary. At the cap, report incomplete work and any proposed follow-up.

Relevant experience includes credit-model risk, underwriting governance,
adverse-action review, or review of model documentation. Record relationships,
compensation, and assistance affecting independence. Either paid or unpaid
participation is possible; payment must not depend on a favorable conclusion.
Organizational affiliation does not imply organizational endorsement.

## Inspect and assess

Read the target's project brief, method, limitations, and two selected reports.
Use its linked source records as needed. Record how much navigation or help
was needed; difficulty finding an answer is itself useful feedback.

1. **Trace a reason.** Follow the target decision from its supplied driver and
   mapping to the recorded reason, rendered notice, and QA result. Identify
   exact record IDs and any break or ambiguity in the chain.
2. **Assess a review gap.** Use the governance report to identify a control
   that is incomplete or not independently assessed. Explain what its status
   permits you to conclude and what remains unknown.
3. **Specify the next action.** For either task, state what you would request
   or inspect next, who would need to provide it, and what would resolve the
   concern. Do not assume the supplied records prove the underlying model.
4. **Assess practical fit.** Explain where the method and pack structure could
   help your stated review context, what is unnecessary or missing, barriers
   to implementation, and whether a simpler approach would suffice.

These are disclosed examples, not a blinded test of defect discovery. Separate
what you observed while doing a task from your opinion about potential use.
No usefulness conclusion is required. Critical, mixed, inconclusive, or
document-only responses are valid when labeled with their actual scope.

## Short response

Use this form or an equivalent response. The long technical execution record
is not required. A reviewer can return their own dated notes with these facts.

- Review ID/date, reviewer identifier, and relevant expertise:
- Personal or organizational capacity; relevant relationships, compensation,
  assistance, and implementation involvement affecting independence:
- Protocol, brief, and target-sheet revisions; software commit or source
  identity; prepared-output references and whether independently regenerated:
- Agreed context, tasks, effort cap, and deliverables; actual effort and omissions:

| Task | Exact artifacts and record IDs examined | Outcome, difficulty, and help needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reason trace | [references] | [completed / partial / not undertaken; observations] |
| Review gap and next action | [references] | [observations and information still needed] |
| Practical fit and barriers | [references] | [reasoned assessment; distinguish opinion from observed use] |

- Findings, if any: stable ID, artifact reference, observed problem or
  limitation, consequence, and what would resolve it. Add severity for defects
  where appropriate under the [shared definitions](protocol.md#findings-response-and-verification).
- Your conclusion: what worked, what did not, and what cannot be concluded:
- Review status: completed within scope / partial / blocked / withdrawn;
  confirm this record accurately describes your work:
- Sharing permission: intended recipients/destination, permitted attribution
  or excerpts, material qualifications, restrictions, and permission date.
  Leave ungranted permissions explicit; completion does not grant permission.

The maintainer records responses and any follow-up separately under the
[shared procedure](protocol.md#findings-response-and-verification), preserving
the original conclusion, adverse findings, and unresolved items. No additional
work or retest is assumed without agreement.

This assessment can support conclusions about the examined artifacts and
tasks. It does not establish verified software behavior, institutional
adoption, effectiveness across institutions, or legal compliance. Public
summaries must retain scope, assistance, material qualifications, and relevant
conflicts or compensation. Keep detailed administrative records separate.
