# Reviewer groups

These three groups describe complementary expertise for assessing this
project's model-governance records, adverse-action reason traceability,
monitoring controls, and review documentation. Use these names when agreeing
an assignment and recording who assessed which aspects of the work.
The project uses synthetic examples; an assessment must retain that limitation.

| Group | Relevant experience | Review focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Technical Implementation Reviewers** | Hands-on credit-model validation, Python-based risk analysis, or software/research engineering with relevant domain knowledge | Execute workflows, inspect implementation, construct independent challenge cases, and assess reproducibility and output correctness. |
| **Credit Governance and Adverse-Action Reviewers** | Model-risk oversight, adverse-action review, credit QA, underwriting operations, or compliance review of credit records | Assess the appropriateness of controls and findings, reconstruct decisions, identify missing information, and evaluate review tasks and next actions. |
| **Methodology and Evaluation Reviewers** | Academic or applied research in credit ML, explanation evaluation, statistics, econometrics, or empirical model governance | Assess the contribution, assumptions, reference answers, comparison methods, evaluation metrics, and whether conclusions follow from the evidence. |

Relevant experience determines fit. Academic researchers, industry
professionals, and independent specialists may qualify for more than one group.
Record each person's actual work and conclusion separately. Multiple roles
held by one person do not constitute multiple independent reviews.

## Match the group to an assignment

- Technical Implementation Reviewers use the
  [scoped technical review](technical-review.md) for execution and challenge
  testing. Where technical and domain expertise are split, identify each
  participant's responsibility.
- Credit Governance and Adverse-Action Reviewers use the
  [practitioner assessment](practitioner-assessment.md) for prepared-record
  inspection and review tasks. Distinguish professional opinion from observed
  task performance, and record any assistance.
- Methodology and Evaluation Reviewers agree a specific methods assessment
  under the [common agreement](protocol.md#target-and-common-agreement).
  Identify the claims, materials, questions, and expected response. Use the
  technical brief when software execution is included; a methods-only
  assessment does not establish independently verified software behavior.

Every assignment identifies an [exact source target](current.md), scope,
deliverables, and effort cap. The group names do not imply review of the whole
repository, a completed assessment, institutional endorsement, or legal
compliance. Follow the [shared procedure](protocol.md) for findings, responses,
and permissions.
