# Limitations

This run kit is a public documentation profile for credit-union review of
AI-enabled underwriting vendors. It should be cited carefully.

## What It Is

- A practical mapping from public NCUA AI, third-party risk, compliance, and
  CUSO guidance themes to the repository's governance artifacts.
- A reviewer-facing checklist for AI vendor due diligence, adverse-action reason
  review, ongoing monitoring, issue escalation, and reviewer signoff.
- A way to ask better questions of a vendor, model-risk reviewer, compliance
  reviewer, counsel, or internal-audit function.

## What It Is Not

- NCUA approval, recognition, endorsement, or validation.
- A legal opinion or compliance certification.
- A substitute for credit-union-specific counsel, compliance, model validation,
  cybersecurity review, vendor-management review, or board oversight.
- Evidence that any credit union, CUSO, bank, fintech, regulator, or vendor has
  adopted this repository.
- Evidence that a real adverse-action notice workflow is accurate.
- Evidence that a real AI vendor is safe, sound, compliant, fair, secure, or
  reliable.

## Scope Limits

The profile is limited to AI or ML-enabled third-party tools used in
small-business or member-business credit underwriting and related
documentation. It does not attempt to cover every credit-union AI use case.

Out-of-scope or adjacent areas include:

- general member-service chatbots
- deposit-account screening
- cryptocurrency or digital-asset services
- identity verification or fraud tools that do not affect credit underwriting
- servicing and collections automation
- marketing-only AI systems
- general enterprise AI governance

Those areas may require related controls, but they should not be cited as
covered by this run kit unless the tool directly affects a covered credit
underwriting decision or its documentation.

## Data and Evidence Limits

The repository's examples use synthetic demonstration data and clearly scoped
public-data run kits. The credit-union profile does not add real credit-union
data, private vendor documentation, production logs, examiner workpapers,
complaint records, or actual adverse-action notices.

The profile can support a future real-world review only if a credit union,
vendor, CUSO, lender, or qualified reviewer supplies appropriate evidence and
permission to use it.

## Fair-Lending and Bias-Risk Limits

Fair-lending, bias-risk, and less-discriminatory-alternative sections are
screening and governance-review prompts. They are not legal conclusions.

Any review involving protected-class data, proxy methods, comparative file
review, state law, federal law, or less-discriminatory-alternative analysis
requires qualified compliance, legal, and model-risk review.

## Security Limits

The data-security and secure-deployment questions in this run kit are
high-level review prompts. They do not replace a security architecture review,
penetration test, SOC report review, incident-response tabletop, privacy review,
or vendor cybersecurity assessment.

## External Validation Gap

This run kit is self-authored. It becomes stronger evidence only if a qualified
external reviewer evaluates the exact artifact, identifies its practical value
or limitations, and provides permission to cite the review.

Useful external reviewers include:

- credit-union compliance officers
- vendor-management or third-party-risk reviewers
- member-business lending leaders
- model-risk or validation practitioners
- consumer-finance or Regulation B counsel
- CUSO risk or compliance leaders
- AI vendor governance or product-risk reviewers
