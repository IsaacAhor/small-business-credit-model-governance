# Release v0.11.0 - Executable Model Governance and Vendor Oversight

Status: synthetic governance-control and third-party oversight milestone.

Release date: 2026-08-23

Git tag: [v0.11.0](https://github.com/IsaacAhor/small-business-credit-model-governance/tree/v0.11.0)

## What This Release Adds

- Adds four linked formal-governance records for model risk and materiality,
  explainability methods, validation, and monitoring plans.
- Enforces reviewer-independence, evidence, finding, disposition, promotion,
  method-population, limitation, threshold, and monitoring relationships.
- Adds six vendor-model oversight contracts covering the review record,
  components, limitations, monitoring configuration, material events, and
  conditional business-credit notice controls.
- Validates evidence presence and cross-record links and fails closed on
  unresolved transparency, compensating-control, heightened-monitoring,
  material-event, and notice-reason gaps.
- Adds seven deterministic synthetic vendor scenarios, including baseline,
  opaque-component, material-change, incident-escalation, notice-gap, and
  intentionally invalid cases.
- Generates deterministic governance and vendor reviewer reports, summaries,
  open-finding registers, and SHA-256 manifests without converting self-review
  into approval.
- Packages the new schemas and exposes installed governance-review,
  vendor-validation, and vendor-report commands.
- Adds a section-by-section FinRegLab implementation crosswalk while preserving
  current SR 26-2, Regulation B, applicability, and nonbinding-source limits.

## Interpretation Boundary

The formal-governance and vendor records, evidence, reviewers, models, and
events in this release are synthetic. The public SBA run kit remains a bounded
approved-loan monitoring demonstration from the prior release.

This release does not establish independent validation, model accuracy or
fitness for use, notice legal sufficiency, vendor reliability, institutional
adoption or reliance, production deployment, legal compliance, regulatory
approval, field recognition, examiner acceptance, or realized impact. A
configured cadence, threshold, residual-risk decision, or signoff remains a
review input rather than a universal requirement or legal conclusion.
