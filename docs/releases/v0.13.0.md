## Changes

- Adds `credit-gov assess-reason-content` and `credit-gov export-reviewed-reasons`.
  Missing checks, unresolved reason findings, incomplete or stale declared
  reviews, and open material defects block a reviewed snapshot export.
- Separates content checks from operational authorization. Diagnostic generation,
  monitoring, benchmark acceptance, hash verification and review exports retain
  explicit scope labels. Even a content pass reports authorization as not provided.
- Detects omitted or unmapped principal drivers in the declared coverage scope,
  including cases where a lower-ranked mapped reason is generated. Mapping and
  notice-template lifecycle checks use the recorded decision event, with
  inclusive activation and exclusive retirement boundaries.
- Generates compatible source-to-notice records when the complete configuration
  is supplied, and rejects partial configuration instead of silently reducing
  the record mode. Generation remains diagnostic and does not invent a reason
  for an unmapped driver or create a review.
- Captures the exact source and input bytes used, including effective schemas
  and applicable external references. Freshness checks reject detected changes
  during a run. These are fingerprints, not authenticated attestations or locks.
- Stages and verifies benchmark replacement before installation, preserves the
  previous pack on failure, and retains recovery material if rollback cannot
  complete. Regression tests cover failure and recovery paths.
- Updates all three review packets for this source and preserves the historical
  v0.12.0 target disclosures and review assignments.

## Migration and compatibility

Use Python 3.10 or later. Reinstall from the v0.13.0 wheel or exact source to
receive these changes; existing installations and v0.12.0 downloads are unchanged.

Decision timestamps must be calendar-valid UTC values in
`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ` form. Normalize offsets explicitly before ingestion;
application dates are not substitutes. Previously accepted malformed, naive,
offset or fractional-second values require correction.

Generation with source-to-notice inputs requires the complete configuration.
Basic generated records cannot satisfy the content gate. Provide the actual
rendered reason segments separately and complete review after inputs are final.

The content contract is `reason-content-review-v4`. Preserve earlier local
`final-use-review.json` records outside the active dataset, resolve their defect
entries, update manifest references and obtain a fresh content review. Renaming
an old record or copying its digest does not migrate approval. Any input-byte or
bound control change requires reassessment. Earlier local final-use command
names are unsupported. See the
[content review and migration contract](https://github.com/IsaacAhor/small-business-credit-model-governance/blob/v0.13.0/docs/adverse-action-reason-run-kit/REASON_CONTENT_REVIEW.md).

## Verification and limits

The release process checks repository and review-target validation, the Python
3.10-3.13 test matrix, source and installed-package behavior, distribution
inspection, SHA-256 checksums and downloaded assets. The suite contains 302 tests;
the four technical-packet test files contain 83. These are maintainer tests on
controlled examples, not independent validation or real-world accuracy estimates.

The synthetic benchmark retains deliberate defects and expects 21 exception
types. Aggregate acceptance is not per-case accuracy, a passing content review,
or permission to issue a notice. Prepared pack provenance records the actual
captured source and inputs; it does not claim a post-tag execution.

Operate with trusted substantive review, a trusted local process and clock,
stable source and inputs, and one writer per destination. Review exports are
historical snapshots. Authenticated reviewer identities and roles, tamper-evident
version/use-bound approvals, authoritative revocation and replay protection,
and coordinated authorization at actual use remain deferred.

This release provides no operational authorization or notice delivery. It does
not establish model-explanation faithfulness, real-world notice accuracy,
regulatory compliance, independent validation, institutional adoption, or
production readiness. Source-archive validation and Windows path limitations
remain documented in the target sheet.
