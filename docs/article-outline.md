# Article Outline

## Working Title

Interpretable Model Risk Monitoring for Machine-Learning-Based Small Business Credit Underwriting: Explainability, Fair Lending, and Governance Applications

## Article Goal

This article should translate the broader repository objective into a serious practitioner-facing or publication-ready written output. It should show that the work is operational, governance-oriented, and directly tied to small business credit underwriting rather than generic AI commentary.

## Core Thesis

Machine-learning-based small business underwriting systems require a structured monitoring approach that integrates model performance, explainability quality, fair-lending oversight, and governance escalation rather than treating each issue as a separate control function.

## Research / Analysis Problem

Existing discussions of AI governance often remain too broad to guide practical post-deployment oversight in small business credit underwriting. A more specific monitoring framework is needed to help institutions identify drift, explanation failures, disparity indicators, and governance breakdowns in a coordinated way.
